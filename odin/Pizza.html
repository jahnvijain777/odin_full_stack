Overview

Homemade pizza has a crisp, golden crust topped with rich tomato sauce, stretchy mozzarella, and flavorful toppings. It is a versatile dish that can be adapted to suit any taste.

Ingredients

  • 1 pizza dough ball
  • 1/2 cup tomato sauce
  • 1 cup shredded mozzarella
  • Your choice of vegetables or other toppings
  • Olive oil and dried oregano

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 230°C (450°F).
  2. Stretch the dough on a floured surface and spread tomato sauce over it.
  3. Add mozzarella, toppings, oregano, and a drizzle of olive oil.
  4. Bake for 12 to 15 minutes, until the crust is golden and the cheese is bubbling.
