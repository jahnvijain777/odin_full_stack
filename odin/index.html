Fluffy Pancakes

Fluffy pancakes are a comforting breakfast favorite with a soft, tender center and lightly golden edges.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 2 tablespoons sugar
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • 3/4 cup milk
  • 1 egg and 2 tablespoons melted butter

Instructions

  1. Whisk the flour, sugar, and baking powder in a bowl.
  2. Stir in the milk, egg, and melted butter until just combined.
  3. Cook portions on a greased skillet until golden.
  4. Serve warm with your favorite toppings.

Sambhar Dosa

Sambhar dosa combines a crisp, golden dosa with tangy, spicy sambhar and coconut chutney.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup rice
  • 1/4 cup urad dal
  • 1/4 teaspoon fenugreek seeds
  • Salt to taste
  • Water and oil as needed

Instructions

  1. Soak the rice, urad dal, and fenugreek seeds for 4 to 6 hours.
  2. Grind, combine, salt, and ferment the batter for 8 to 12 hours.
  3. Spread the batter on a hot skillet and cook until crisp.
  4. Serve with sambhar and coconut chutney.

Classic Lasagna

A hearty baked dish made with layers of pasta, seasoned meat sauce, creamy ricotta, and melted cheese.

Ingredients

  • Lasagna sheets
  • 500 g ground beef
  • 2 cups tomato sauce
  • 1 cup ricotta and 2 cups mozzarella
  • 1/2 cup grated Parmesan

Instructions

  1. Brown the beef, add tomato sauce, and simmer for 15 minutes.
  2. Layer sauce, pasta, ricotta, and mozzarella in a baking dish.
  3. Finish with mozzarella and Parmesan.
  4. Bake at 190°C (375°F) for 35 to 40 minutes.

Garlic Tomato Pasta

A quick Italian-inspired meal made with al dente pasta, bright tomato sauce, garlic, and fresh herbs.

Ingredients

  • 200 g pasta
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 3 minced garlic cloves
  • 2 cups crushed tomatoes
  • Salt, pepper, and fresh basil

Instructions

  1. Cook the pasta in salted water according to the package directions.
  2. Sauté garlic in olive oil, then simmer with tomatoes.
  3. Toss the pasta with the sauce and season to taste.
  4. Garnish with basil and serve hot.
