Overview

Fluffy pancakes are a comforting breakfast favorite with a soft, tender center and lightly golden edges. They are quick to prepare and taste delicious with fruit, butter, maple syrup, or other favorite toppings.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 2 tablespoons sugar
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • 3/4 cup milk
  • 1 egg and 2 tablespoons melted butter

Instructions

  1. Whisk the flour, sugar, and baking powder in a bowl.
  2. Stir in the milk, egg, and melted butter until just combined.
  3. Cook small portions on a greased skillet until bubbles form, then flip and cook until golden.
  4. Serve warm with your favorite toppings.
