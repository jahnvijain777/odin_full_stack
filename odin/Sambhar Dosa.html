Overview

Sambhar dosa is a popular South Indian meal that combines a crisp, golden dosa with tangy, spicy sambhar. The dosa is made from fermented rice and urad dal batter and is traditionally served with coconut chutney.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup rice
  • 1/4 cup urad dal
  • 1/4 teaspoon fenugreek seeds
  • Salt to taste
  • Water and oil as needed

Instructions

  1. Soak the rice, urad dal, and fenugreek seeds separately for 4 to 6 hours.
  2. Grind them with water into a smooth batter, combine, add salt, and ferment for 8 to 12 hours.
  3. Stir the fermented batter and add water if needed to make it pourable.
  4. Spread a ladleful on a hot skillet, drizzle with oil, and cook until crisp and golden.
  5. Serve hot with sambhar and coconut chutney.
