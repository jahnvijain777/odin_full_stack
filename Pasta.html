Overview

Garlic tomato pasta is a simple, flavorful Italian-inspired meal made with al dente pasta, a bright tomato sauce, fragrant garlic, and fresh herbs. It is quick enough for a weeknight dinner and easy to customize.

Ingredients

  • 200 g pasta
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 3 minced garlic cloves
  • 2 cups crushed tomatoes
  • Salt, pepper, and fresh basil

Instructions

  1. Cook the pasta in salted water according to the package directions.
  2. Sauté the garlic in olive oil, then add the tomatoes and simmer for 10 minutes.
  3. Toss the drained pasta with the sauce and season to taste.
  4. Garnish with basil and serve hot.
