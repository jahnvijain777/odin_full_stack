Classic Lasagna

Overview

Classic lasagna is a hearty Italian-inspired baked dish made with layers of pasta, seasoned meat sauce, creamy ricotta, and melted cheese. It is a satisfying meal that is ideal for sharing with family and friends.

Ingredients

  • Lasagna sheets
  • 500 g ground beef
  • 2 cups tomato sauce
  • 1 cup ricotta and 2 cups shredded mozzarella
  • 1/2 cup grated Parmesan

Instructions

  1. Brown the beef, stir in the tomato sauce, and simmer for 15 minutes.
  2. Layer sauce, pasta, ricotta, and mozzarella in a baking dish.
  3. Repeat the layers and finish with mozzarella and Parmesan.
  4. Bake at 190°C (375°F) for 35 to 40 minutes, then rest before serving.
